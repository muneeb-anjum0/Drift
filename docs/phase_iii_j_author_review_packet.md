## Authoring boundary

Muneeb will supply newly authored cases and his human review, as requested. This is **self-review, not independent adjudication by a separate reviewer**; preserve that limitation. Author from the aggregate target descriptions below, not from any Phase III-D/E/I/I.5 case or individual model error. Do not open or paraphrase those closed cases while writing the new corpus. The old V5 simulated archive is historical data, not a fresh authored holdout.

Each row needs a unique `id`; one `baseline_requirement`; one `message`; one proposed canonical label (`added`, `modified`, `removed`, `contradiction`, `ambiguous`, `unchanged`); a semantic/template `family_id`; a `domain`; a proposed `partition` (`train`, `development`, `final`); author and authored date; license/source note; privacy classification; and explicit `kaggle_upload_approved` (`YES` or `NO`). Family IDs must cover paraphrases, minimal variants, shared semantic skeletons, and domain-transformed variants. Put **every member of a family in the same partition**. Final-holdout cases should be separately authored and should not be shown to a data generator, trainer, or model-selection process. A `NO` row cannot enter an external-upload training ZIP.

Author across unrelated domains and writing styles. Primary boundaries are **removed vs modified** (including partial removal, permission/channel/scope/data removal, and genuine surviving-behavior changes) and **contradiction vs modified** (explicit must/must-not/only/never/required/optional/before/after, role and ordering invariants, plus compatible changes). **Added vs modified** is secondary (new actor/channel/data/option versus altered existing behavior). Include independently written unchanged and ambiguous regression controls. Avoid a single telltale word determining the class. Do not copy the old evaluation examples into this packet, even with substituted names or numbers.

The review file needs one decision per case: `CONFIRMED`, `REVISED`, `AMBIGUOUS`, or `EXCLUDE`. For `CONFIRMED` or `REVISED`, provide the reviewed canonical label. For `REVISED`, `AMBIGUOUS`, or `EXCLUDE`, give a short reason. A review decision `AMBIGUOUS` means there is **no defensible unique six-class truth** and the row will be unscored; this differs from a valid canonical `ambiguous` label. Provide reviewer name, review date, and a truthful provenance statement. The original proposal and review will both be preserved. The current validator requires at least one scored example of each class in train and development and **at least 20 scored examples of each class in the protected final partition**; it will stop if this coverage is not met rather than inventing labels or relaxing a gate after evaluation.

## Freeze and Kaggle separation

After receipt, the validator will check unique IDs, coverage, labels, review reasons, family-disjoint partitions, duplicate/near-overlap and closed-set contamination proxies, source/privacy metadata, and exact hashes. Failed review or material overlap **stops the process**, rather than silently rewriting labels or cases. Only the reviewed train/development payload and a **hash/count seal** for the final partition will be committed before training. The final case text stays outside Git and the training ZIP until candidate selection and the acceptance gate are frozen.

The Kaggle **training ZIP must contain train/development data and frozen scripts/configuration, but not the protected final holdout**. Keep the final holdout separately sealed until a single candidate and the gate are frozen; then perform the old-vs-new raw comparison once. Do not use final cases for early stopping, repair, hyperparameter choice, or a follow-up training iteration. The package will omit GGUF/base weights/checkpoints and will fail closed if hashes, GPU memory, dependencies, or files do not match its manifest. Upload and execution on Kaggle remain user actions; no paid compute or external upload is initiated by this repository. Use a **private** Kaggle dataset for non-public inputs. [Kaggle's dataset documentation](https://www.kaggle.com/docs/datasets) says ZIP uploads are extracted with their directory structure preserved; the executable package must therefore locate its extracted directory rather than assume the ZIP itself remains mounted. [Kaggle's notebook documentation](https://www.kaggle.com/docs/notebooks) identifies `/kaggle/working` as the place for saved output artifacts.

Please provide the completed case and review files, including a distinct proposed final partition. Do not send protected Phase III-I/I.5 cases as substitutes. If available, also state the Kaggle GPU type and available VRAM; the executable package will use a resource preflight rather than assuming a particular accelerator.
