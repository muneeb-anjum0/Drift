## Corpus design, quotas, and metadata

Target 204 cases; acceptable reviewed/scorable range 180–240 **if quality permits**. Do not add filler to satisfy a number. Each of six canonical labels should have at least 20 reviewed/scorable cases; class balance need not be exact. Use at least 15 unrelated domains, with no domain above 12% of scored cases. Each of the four preassigned difficulty tiers should contain at least 30 cases. T1 = one clear operation; T2 = composition or context with one discernible operation; T3 = semantic boundary/lexical trap/negation/partial removal; T4 = realistic messy user, product, or developer language. Tiers are assigned before inference and never changed based on predictions.

Each case contains immutable ID, baseline requirement, message, proposed label and short semantic rationale, tier, semantic family, domain, requirement structure (`atomic`, `compound`, `conditional`, `role`, `numeric`, `temporal`, `multi_option`, `negative_invariant`), length bucket (`short` ≤20 words, `medium` 21–60, `long` 61–160), and phenomenon tags. Length is measured over baseline plus message, excluding metadata. Longer cases must still fit the actual 768-token runtime and existing prompt/output guards. At least 36 cases must be naturalistic/messy, 30 must involve partial removal, 30 matched genuine modifications, 24 lexical traps, 24 negation, and 12 each numeric, role/permission, and temporal semantics. These tags overlap; none is an exclusive stratum. Include `not`, `never`, `no longer`, `except`, `unless`, `only`, `must not`, `cannot`, `without`, `anything but`, `neither/nor`, and `instead of` where natural, without making a word alone determine the label.

Predeclare at least 18 two-item minimal-pair families spanning removed/modified, modified/added, modified/contradiction, modified/unchanged, modified/ambiguous, added/unchanged, removed/contradiction, and added/contradiction; each boundary gets at least one family. At least 10 paraphrase families have ≥3 same-label members, at least 12 distractor pairs hold the semantic request fixed, and at least 12 order pairs reorder independent clauses. A case may belong to more than one analysis family only when the relation remains unambiguous. Family IDs and expected relation are frozen with the corpus. These quotas are design targets; report any shortfall as a limitation, not as a retroactive rule change.

## Canonical single-label ontology

`unchanged`: supplied requirement is semantically unaffected. `added`: new capability, option, actor, channel, or data item while baseline capability remains. `removed`: an explicit baseline capability, option, permission, or scope item is eliminated even if other behavior survives. `modified`: existing behavior survives but timing, value, format, access, or rule changes. `contradiction`: new request is incompatible with an explicit must/must-not/only/never/required/optional/before/after invariant in the baseline. `ambiguous`: intent or constraints are insufficient, unresolved, hypothetical, or internally conflicting. A question is not automatically ambiguous; wording and decisional intent matter. These boundaries are inherited from Phase III-I, not new P1 instructions. Do not invent precedence for a message containing different simultaneous operations. Such cases are ontology-gap diagnostics outside six-class primary scoring unless existing canonical semantics uniquely resolves them. Preserve and report those cases rather than forcing labels.

## Review and contamination gate

Author proposals **without model predictions**. Then perform exact pair, exact baseline/message, normalized pair, numeric-masked template, family/template grouping, and high lexical-similarity checks within the new corpus and against available historical training data. Compare against closed D/E/I only through an overlap tool after candidate authoring. Flag possible semantic overlap for independent manual assessment without exposing closed cases to the case author. Exclude or replace contaminated new cases **before prediction**, preserving an exclusion ledger and original candidate hash. Do not claim absence of contamination from a limited check.

An actual human reviewer, blind to model output, judges every proposed case `CONFIRMED`, `REVISED`, `AMBIGUOUS`, or `EXCLUDE`, documenting reasons for the latter three. Preserve proposed and reviewed labels. `AMBIGUOUS`/`EXCLUDE` and ontology-gap cases are not scored as one of six classes; report counts and reasons. The reviewer must evaluate semantic correctness, not difficulty. Do not claim independence that was not observed. If review is unavailable, provenance is unclear, or the validator fails, **stop before inference** and report `EVALUATION INCONCLUSIVE`; an AI/advisory review is not represented as independent human review. Hash and commit the reviewed scoring corpus and manifest before the first call. Frozen truth, tiers, tags, family links, and exclusions cannot be edited after predictions begin. No post-hoc acceptable-label sets.

## Fixed analysis and interpretation

Primary: raw correct/total, accuracy, macro precision/recall/F1, per-class support/correct/precision/recall/F1, six-by-six confusion matrix, invalid JSON/schema/label/missing-field/parser counts, and full error ledger. Every percentage is accompanied by N. Slices: class, tier, domain, length, requirement structure, negation, numeric, role, temporal, partial removal, lexical traps, naturalistic language. Report tiny slices as diagnostic only. Robustness: minimal pairs `both correct / first only / second only / neither`; paraphrase family unanimity and individual flip rate; distractor and order invariance; and metamorphic relation consistency, each with denominator. For invariant pairs, both predictions must match; for contrastive pairs, both must satisfy the predeclared relation. Confidence bands and high-confidence errors may be reported, but self-reported confidence is **not calibrated probability**. PP1 fixes/worsens/neutral and postprocessed metrics are separate from raw metrics. Stability is exact label agreement on the 24 preselected repeats.

Assign every raw semantic error a post-hoc primary taxonomy and optional contributing tags: `REMOVAL_AS_MODIFICATION`, `MODIFICATION_AS_REMOVAL`, `CONTRADICTION_MISSED`, `FALSE_CONTRADICTION`, `AMBIGUITY_MISSED`, `FALSE_AMBIGUITY`, `ADDITION_BOUNDARY_ERROR`, `UNCHANGED_BOUNDARY_ERROR`, `NEGATION_FAILURE`, `SCOPE_FAILURE`, `ROLE_FAILURE`, `NUMERIC_FAILURE`, `TEMPORAL_FAILURE`, `DISTRACTOR_SENSITIVITY`, `ORDER_SENSITIVITY`, `PARAPHRASE_INSTABILITY`, `COMPOSITION_FAILURE`, `OTHER` with explanation. Do not reinterpret a disputed reviewed label based on model agreement. Stop the run for hash/runtime mismatch, invalid review/freeze, unexpected GPU configuration, resource guard breach, or materially compromised data; checkpoint partial results and report inconclusive. Do not stop early because scores look good or bad. No single accuracy pass threshold.

Final decision is exactly one of `TARGETED RETRAINING SCOPE CONFIRMED`, `TARGETED RETRAINING SCOPE REVISED`, `RETRAINING EVIDENCE WEAKENED`, or `EVALUATION INCONCLUSIVE`. The first requires recurring independently reviewed, stable, cross-domain learnable boundary errors consistent with III-I; the second requires similarly credible errors but a materially different dominant failure surface; the third requires valid evidence that III-I's proposed emphasis does not generalize; the fourth applies if provenance, ontology, contamination, structure, or runtime prevents a credible judgment. Compare rates and raw counts by boundary/tier, not merely one aggregate; distinguish data ambiguity and failure of the model. Produce an aggregate training-target matrix (observed N, errors, rate, severity, systematicity, likely trainability, recommended emphasis) and update the III-J proposal from aggregate findings only. No training starts in this phase.
