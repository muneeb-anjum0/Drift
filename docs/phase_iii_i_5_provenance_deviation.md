# Phase III-I.5 authoring-provenance deviation

This is a pre-inference disclosure. While inspecting how the previous freeze validator and draft were structured, the Phase III-I proposed draft's first 110 lines were displayed to the Phase III-I.5 case author. This exposed a few individual **proposed** III-I cases before the new candidate corpus was authored. The Phase III-I raw predictions and error ledger were not opened for this purpose, and no individual failed case was intentionally copied, paraphrased, or mutated into the new corpus. Nonetheless the author was **not fully blind to closed Phase III-I case text**, contrary to the strongest reading of the preregistered closed-corpus procedure.

The new 216-case candidate has zero exact normalized pair matches and zero same-baseline message-Jaccard≥0.85 matches against the available III-I reviewed corpus (and the other available closed finals), but lexical screens do not prove semantic independence. The candidate remains **provisional, unreviewed, and unscored**. Its review packet discloses this issue. Before any inference, an independent reviewer should assess whether this limited exposure materially compromises the corpus. If it does, the affected or entire candidate must be replaced by an author who has not seen closed III-I cases, with exclusions and replacement provenance recorded before a new freeze. No post-prediction repair is permitted.

The methodology commit `922ee58` and its original rules remain unchanged. This deviation is recorded separately rather than silently weakening or rewriting the preregistration. The prior III-I human review also remains user-self-attested, not independently witnessed by this agent.
